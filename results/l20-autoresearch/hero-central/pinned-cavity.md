They must not come from the reserved production/issuance or any previous embargo. First start family: permutations of the prescribed inventory independent of the boundary interior. Second: independent within-inventory starts deliberately far from the first in packing/energy, with construction frozen before draws. Repeated measurements of one trajectory do not count as independent interior replicas. A single bulk interior today cannot certify conditional recurrence.

## Power and prospective decision

Let Y=f(V), v=Var_fit(f)>0 (frozen), d(B)=m1(B,I)−m0(B,I). For one independently equilibrated replica,

`D = [(Y−m0)²−(Y−m1)²]/v = d(B)[2Y−m0−m1]/v`.

The primary effect is E[D]. The useful floor is **0.01**, powered at a prespecified alternative **0.02**, using a two-sided 95% interval and 90% power. Independent-boundary and within-boundary (thermal) variance components are `σB² = Var_B(E[D|B])` and `σW² = E_B Var(D|B)`. For J independent boundaries and r independent replicas each, `Var(mean D)=(σB²+σW²/r)/J`; even infinite r cannot remove σB². Estimate σW² from within-boundary sample variances; estimate σB² from variance of boundary means minus σW²/r. Retain any negative raw estimate in the receipt; truncate only for planning, not as evidence of zero heterogeneity. Protect pilot uncertainty by a whole-boundary resampling upper 95% variance estimate for each proposed r. Report cross-boundary and within-boundary variances of **both** Y and D; they need not have the same ratios.

For r in {4,8,16,32,64}, choose J using the one-sample noncentral-t power for (0.02−0.01)/sqrt(σB²+σW²/r), then round up. Use at least 32 independent boundaries. Select the affordable pair minimizing J(cBoundary+r·cReplica) from measured pilot costs, **before unblinding**. The continuous cost-optimal r is sqrt(cBoundary·σW²/(cReplica·σB²)) when both variances are positive. Independently power the ordered high/low mean difference using disjoint *pairs* and their paired variances in units of frozen SD(f); null zero, target +0.10 SD(f), 90% power. Require the lower confidence bound >0 and report fraction of correctly ordered pairs (a replica-noisy descriptive statistic). The two requirements are conjunctive, not two chances to pass. A 256-boundary issue cohort allows at most 128 disjoint pairs *before matching*. If either powered design exceeds the available independent boundaries or the authorized cost, add neither substitute sites nor copies of exteriors: report insufficient power/request genuinely new independent exteriors. No resource allocation for this protocol is currently authorized.

For exact I, conditional independence of two replicas gives Cov(Y₁,Y₂|I) = Var_B(E[Y|B,I]|I). The conditional-memory fraction is the inventory-weighted numerator divided by E_I Var(Y|I). Uncentered replica products mix inventory fluctuations with boundary memory. If exact-inventory support is insufficient, this fraction is unidentified; a cross-fitted inventory regression gives a model-dependent sensitivity only. The thin shell can capture at most the part forecastable from its own information and I, not necessarily the variance explained by the full pinned exterior. Compare f to standard overlaps, coordination, packing and crystal readouts on the same converged cavities; never promote a secondary observable after looking.

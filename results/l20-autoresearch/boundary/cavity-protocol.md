# Can a particular frozen exterior select a vacancy tripod instead of a T?

**Prospective experiment, not performed.** The L20 endpoint release contains one bulk realization per independent parent. The original [production protocol](https://github.com/konradha/lattice-exploration/blob/5df879576cede333bd14eac07f8578b01b9a7e12/runs/l20-b305-production-v1/design/protocol.json) describes local whole-system trajectories, not independent interiors equilibrated against the same frozen exterior. Neither rotations of a sample nor frames of those trajectories supply the missing replicas. The [point-to-set construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.03573) motivates pinning; the endpoint here is discrimination between two explicit arrangements rather than a generic overlap.

**Two arrangements.** Vacant center, three vacancies and three particles on its six nearest sites. The vacancies either lie on three distinct axes (**tripod**) or include an opposite pair (**T**). These two classes exhaust the 20 orientation patterns (8 and 12 respectively); neither class is counted as 50% by definition. Match the sorted occupied-neighbor coordination of the three particles *exactly* to the one signature selected on discovery parents. Both arrangements thus have the same local density, central coordination and particle coordination census on the cross. This does not fix bonds outside the cross, hidden species, or the entire interior energy.

**What is issued.** At β=3.05, use 1,024 distinct prespecified archived parents, one center each, picked without reading any of their interior outcomes in this study. The encoder reads the 386 vacancy bits in the Chebyshev shell at radius 4 only. It never sees the matched signature, original center, interior counts, or outer species as an input. Everyone predicts the same conditional probability `p_b = P(tripod | tripod or T with the fixed signature, boundary b)`, not the unconditional frequency of tripods. Freeze all 1,024 scores, baseline scores, canonical archive rows and anchors, encoder and selection hashes; never replace an issued boundary. Freeze predictions even if the static gate is null, but do not recommend expensive validation of a failed archive predictor. The static score uses one correlated bulk interior, not recurring arrangements.

**Packing must get a fair chance.** Controls include shell density, pair/Fourier structure, period-two packing templates over phases and rotations, and the best packing patterns of a 2×2×5 crystal at the true composition: 2,400 A and 3,600 B per 8,000 lattice sites. The latter minimizes the NH energy exactly over all 15,504 five-vacancy unit cells and their optimal allocation of six A labels. Its 2,600 minimizing patterns per long-axis registration have energy 7 per 20 sites. This is a strong *finite family*, not an exhaustive theory of local packing.

**If separately authorized to make cavities.** Pin all three species at all sites with Chebyshev radius ≥4 to their original values. Only the 7³ interior is mobile. The encoder still only gets its radius-4 binary shell. The interaction energy includes changed terms centered outside the mobile core. Parent release hash plus row and anchor identifies the entire exterior; record the exact exterior hash and original interior composition, which must remain fixed separately for each cavity. Replicas share the boundary, β, interior composition and nothing else. Balance independent random-number streams with two independently generated, composition-preserving starting protocols—random and preordered/incompatible. Do not recycle an archived center as an independent outcome, or count walkers, frames or spins as independent boundaries.

**Mixing before scoring.** A separate pilot must determine the physical cost, matched-event frequency and a common equilibration depth without access to which eligible replicas are tripod or T. Use convergence of energy, central vacancy rate, coordination, and overlap between disparate starts at successive doubling depths. Before unblinding any class outcomes, lock the full depth and equal replica count per boundary. If convergence or affordable exposure fails, report *not tested*, not a negative physics result. For production require the two start families to agree: separate simultaneous parent-based 95% intervals for differences in E/site, central vacancy probability and integrated normalized overlap strictly within ±0.005, ±0.03 and ±0.05 respectively at the last two locked depths. Failure invalidates a recurrence claim. Keep all initial transients and observed prevalence.

**Individual-boundary test.** Use one final draw per independent replica. Mark outcomes `tripod`, `T`, or `ineligible`; record every outcome and denominator including zero-event boundaries. For each eligible draw compute log probability under the issued learned and best ordinary predictor on *that same boundary*. Primary: mean paired log-score gain ≥0.001 nat per eligible interior, assessed by a two-sided 95% lower limit from deleting whole independent boundary parents in the ratio of sums. The upper limit below 0.001 excludes that practical advantage, not all selectivity. This weights opportunities by eligibility; do not claim equal-boundary prevalence. Also report by boundary, start family and predicted risk, with calibration and absolute probabilities.

**Recurrence rather than just prevalence.** Without any replica outcomes, split boundaries into eight equal bins sorted by packing forecast; sort within each by `p_learned - p_packing` (ties by canonical row). Pair bottom with top, next-bottom with next-top, and so on; retain the pair identities even if some boundaries never regenerate the matched class. In each start family separately, compare mean `observed tripods − sum(p_packing over eligible draws)` per eligible draw between these high and low residual halves. Require both signed contrasts positive with simultaneous 95% lower limits (Bonferroni over the two start families; resampling pairs of independent boundaries, not replicas). This tests whether specific predicted boundaries select the geometry beyond the packing forecast. Also publish same-boundary across-start agreement versus different-boundary agreement within packing bins and the number of boundaries with eligible draws in both starts. The last diagnostic uses a different, joint-eligibility-weighted population. No replacement, sign flip, radius search or relabeling after scoring.

**Stop/go.** Before requesting any simulation, report archive event frequency, expected eligible endpoints and expected number of distinct repeatedly informative boundaries. A cheap-looking run with almost no eligible interiors is not a usable test. Plan at least 100 boundaries with eligible draws in both start families; otherwise report recurrence as underpowered. Even if both tests pass, the result is a microscopic carrier of boundary information at this β and radius relative to the tested packing models, not a new phase. A packing win is a positive explanation; indistinguishable predictions with tight bounds are a quantitative limit.

**Discovery exposure, before fitting the boundary predictor.** The selected occupied coordinations are **(3,3,5)**. Of 81,920,000 anchors on 10,240 discovery parents, 126,524 were eligible (0.15445%; one in 647), of which 38.36% were tripods. These anchors are not independent observations. At 1,024 uniformly chosen boundaries and 32 replicas per boundary, homogeneous eligibility at that rate would give only **51 eligible endpoints total and 0.61 boundaries with an event in each start family**. At 512 replicas per boundary the corresponding expectations are 810 and 109. The latter calculation is `1024 * [1 − (1 − f)^(replicas/2)]²`; real frozen-boundary propensities can be heterogeneous, so this is an exposure illustration, not a measured cavity power or a recommendation to buy half a million replicas.

If a future pilot measures very low yield, an **additional**, lower-variance estimator is available. In each independently equilibrated cavity, condition on everything except the central seven sites and integrate their finite conditional Boltzmann partition sum analytically at fixed composition. This gives conditional expectations of the separate tripod and matched-event indicators. Average their numerators and denominators over independent interior equilibrations before taking their ratio. The law of iterated expectation keeps the same target without promoting rotations or analytic terms to independent observations. A single conditional sum in the archived interior does **not** re-equilibrate the rest of the cavity. It cannot supply the primary recurrence experiment.

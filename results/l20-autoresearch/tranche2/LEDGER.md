**What survives a baseline?** The fixed total number of A particles predicts *how much* two distant vacancy-bond choices couple. The registered compact three-bond geometry cannot be reduced exactly to any pair potential, but that mathematical difference carries almost no predictive weight in the sampled glass. These findings answer different questions: the first coupling disappears in a common-fugacity bath; the second is computed at each fixed exterior, not from a mixture of exteriors. Neither tests recurrence of an interior under a distant frozen boundary.

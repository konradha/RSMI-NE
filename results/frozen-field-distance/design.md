# Frozen field distance · pre-outcome contract

**Question.** Do three actual stock RSMI contact-cavity encoders, frozen at β=3.05 update 10,218 (6101, 6102, 6103), carry a reproducible signed directional two-point correlation not accounted for by conventional local packing? No new representation, optimizer, temperature tuning, dynamics, GPU or changes to closed gates. Prior [06](../experiment-06-cubic-families/design.json) tested *one-star* cubic content and interseed agreement. [07](../experiment-07-boundary-forecast/design.json) and [cold transfer](../cold-frozen-transfer-320-340/design.json) forecast or enumerate an immediately bounded star. The opening study tested dynamical, not neural, observables. A report/handoff and local interactive prompt index search found no previous identical disjoint-star polarization test. This lock is before native spatial outcomes. We follow the operator-by-correlator approach of [Gökmen et al., PRL 127, 240603](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.11633v3), the symmetry/ensemble analysis of [PRE 104, 064106](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.16887v3) and physical-correlation validation in [Nature Communications 15, 10214](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54341-8); no assumption of a fixed point or a dimer-like phase.

**Population.** Native L20 z,y,x axes, periodic. Every site can be a center; only the exact 03 seven-site 223 sector (2 vacancies, 2 A, 3 B) has labels. Reproduce state/arm order, category map bytes and cubic actions on all 210 states. Source: original 8,192 TRAIN (prior FIT+DEV), 4,096 TEST assessments at β=3.05. β=0 specificity: 4,096 TEST assessments, same frozen fields; no beta-zero fitting. Select admissible rows *before* reading species, no static reserve (4,096 per beta excluded), including TRAIN nuisance estimates. Two independent temperature transfers: all 128 β=3.20 and all 128 β=3.40 single nonlocal 500M preparations, no 300M forks or physical children. Distinct identities against source archive. TEST here is held from this construction, not historically untouched. No other-temperature search. Parent observations are independent units; same parent throughout seeds, axes, distances, null draws and models.

**Field.** Compute proper vector-valued covariants, not a scalar T1u projector: for frozen one-hot labels take the 3×3 cubic matrix units (vector contraction against the active group actions), retaining all originating component copies and categories in the trace. Check map replay and covariance under all 48 transformations, including inversion; joint signed permutations of spatial and component axes. No axis selected from correlations. Orbit-average the source TRAIN one-star mass (each parent contributes a normalized eligible-star histogram) for all normalizations and LS. Normalize total trace variance per physical component to one per model: orientation mean of sum over copy variances, not whitening residuals to unit variance. Report raw total/remaining variance and residual-normalized correlation as well as total-scale residual covariance. All decisions use the latter. The field parity fixes mean in the cubic reference; pair endpoint means are estimated separately below.

**Ordinary fields.** Basic D_i^t = 1[+i=t]−1[−i=t] for vacancy,A,B. Enriched nested span: these vectors, times central species indicators, times the counts across axes of each of six unordered opposite-arm species classes, and axis-specific signed ordered-vs-reversed pair contrasts for unlike opposite-arm types. No higher interactions, exteriors or dynamics. One joint source TRAIN weighted least-squares projection over Cartesian components using invariant coefficients per learned copy, not independently direction-tuned. Compare covariance of total, residual to each span, and ordinary orthonormal packing span fields on fixed TRAIN scale. Covariances of residuals are **not** claimed to equal total minus conventional correlations. Random controls: 06 state-label shuffles PCG64([609006, seed, draw]), 64 per seed, preserve category counts. Before scoring retain the fixed nearest 8 by squared Euclidean difference in TRAIN T1u trace fraction and H/log(4) to that seed; ties by draw number. Report differences; do not call nulls exact entropy/power matches. Mean over their total and enriched-residual covariance, each on its own total TRAIN scale. For a positive specificity claim require absolute difference of the selected-null mean from the real value ≤0.10 on **both** matching coordinates for each seed; otherwise comparison is sensitivity-limited even if the physical correlations pass.

**Spatial estimator.** For each parent, positive PBC axial separations d=3,4,5,6,7,8,9 (no star shares any site). Only pairs jointly eligible at this particular d/axis are in the denominator. Signed within-aperture cross-covariance: subtract the **two distinct endpoint means within parent, axis and d**, n−1 denominator with at least 2 pairs; trace over corresponding vector copies. L uses component parallel to displacement; T averages the two perpendicular components without switching signs. Equal three axes; an unsupported axis makes that distance unavailable for that parent. Single within-parent uniform permutation of 210-state *labels among eligible positions* per parent (unseen states never imputed); preserve mask, one-point field distribution and aperture, apply to every model/axis/d simultaneously. PCG64 SeedSequence [734982, beta_index, uint64 chain_seed, uint64 chain_id]. Report true and shuffled covariance on same pairs and their paired difference. Genuine physical pairs may share sites with *other* pairs, not within a pair; only independent-parent intervals.

**Decision endpoint.** Primary is arithmetic mean of d=5 and d=6 per parent (minimum inter-star site distances 3 and 4, beyond the Hamiltonian term diameter 2), both required. Other d descriptive with pointwise parent intervals, no choosing favorable lag. Entire TRAIN measured descriptively, no TEST-derived normalization, sign, model or exclusions. Use a two-sided fixed family that covers either sign rather than optimizing a sign on a target. For each seed/component/cohort measure (i) enriched residual real R, (ii) enriched R minus its paired label-shuffle null, (iii) (ii) minus the mean shuffle-corrected enriched residual of the eight matched partitions. Family of 3 seeds × 2 components × 4 held cohorts × 3 contrasts = 72 intervals, simultaneous Bonferroni two-sided t critical 1−.05/(2×72), using empirical SD of parent differences/√n. Others pointwise descriptive. Absolute correlations, eigenvector maximization and products of signs are not substitutes. Keep signed d-specific results visible for oscillation; pooling can cancel an oscillatory effect.

Require ≥96 contributing parents and ≥80% of the roster, ≥300 actual jointly eligible pairs for each (d=5,6, axis) per cohort, TRAIN total variance >1e−10 and residual fraction ≥.01 per seed. Precision target simultaneous half-width ≤.01. Minimum interesting effect is .01 on unit total TRAIN component-variance scale. Positive: same L or T component AND same sign across all 3 seeds in held source and BOTH transfers, all three signed interval lower bounds in that direction >.01, null-matching valid, support and precision pass; β=0 corresponding three contrast intervals contained within ±.01. These components are specified jointly, no seed selection or per-cell p-hunting. Equivalence **for residual propagation only** if all real and shuffle-corrected enriched residual intervals for both components and all seeds in source and both transfers are contained within ±.01 with support/variance. Otherwise report strongest bounds and precisely where support, precision, specificity, or transfer falls short. Rejection of this promotion criterion alone is not physical equivalence. No claim about all nonlinear dependence, phases, or other operators.

**Admission.** No held species or pair scoring before frozen design, TRAIN map bank, synthetic weak-coupling/null decision and geometry checks have been published. Weak positive control must recover the prespecified directional sign and approximate size, not merely run. Report seeds, parents, support, scatter and exact errors. If control/qualification fails, report the failure rather than using held data to repair it. Source fitting, validation and reporting are CPU only. Publish measurements and stop regardless of result.

# L20 operator search: discovery and confirmation

Two different questions; neither objective is evidence for the other. All runs local; no pinned-cavity run. The two new physical cohorts are evaluation-only. No hyperparameters or operator changes chosen using their outcomes.

1. **Spatial equilibrium.** β=3.05, periodic blocks of width 3 and 5; buffers 1 and 2; a four-state learned bottleneck and fixed physical codes. Match the number of shell observations. Select the geometry/fit by validation, not by the best test ordinate. Freeze before transfer. Compare held-out bounds, shuffled negatives, seed stability and nonoverlapping spatial correlations. A better bound alone is not a new order parameter. L20 alone cannot establish finite-size scaling.
2. **Space-time.** Train on the previous 64-parent β=3.05 development cohort only. The input to the four-state encoder is static. The environment is future same-species overlap loss on oriented patches *outside* the input. Score the frozen code against both explicit kinetic/target-region controls and equally sized untrained codes. The primary clock is 4,096 local sweeps at β=3.05. The second release, β=2.711111111111, uses 116 sweeps as primary transfer (matched t/τα); 462/16,384 are matched secondary clocks. The transfer cohort also has 4,096 sweeps as a separately labeled equal-clock diagnostic. Use paired estimates across whole independent parents. Do not count forks, sites, directions or repeated lags as independent observations.
3. **What counts as directional information?** Check the spatially matched prediction after subtracting the motif's mean over faces, not only the marginal score. Show sibling-half reproducibility where possible and whether adding the learned field improves on matched physics and random controls. A predictor of how active an entire patch is need not predict *where* changes occur. Activity outside a central motif is an association and can be driven by other nearby defects.
4. **Mechanistic interpretation.** An allowed free swap may simply reverse. A candidate local descriptor is whether hypothetically performing it opens a previously unavailable, non-uphill continuation sharing an endpoint (excluding reversal). This uses no future labels. Treat it as a fixed mechanism/control, not an RSMI-discovered operator without evidence. Do not tune a new mechanism on the fresh outcomes.

Physical acquisition: 512 new independent parents × 8 children at β=3.05; 256 × 8 at β=2.711111111111. Sample production-only rows from the original static test partitions and exclude all old cold development parents. Archive lossless species XOR to each origin, recomputed energies, accepted counts, source identities and checksums. Hold all simulated outcomes separate from fit selection. The earlier β-specific measured τα values are 65,818,718 and 1,857,140 sweeps. All new replicated times are much shorter than relaxation; this does not measure cold relaxation-scale site propensity.

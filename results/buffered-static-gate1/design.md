# Buffered static Gate 1 — pre-outcome lock

Decision question: does a buffered shell improve a **probability forecast of the native 210-state star**, relative to conventional shell packing and both crystals? Not an RSMI score, not conditional mutual information. Gate 2, local-only trajectories, kinetics, new physics simulations and all paid compute are locked. Stock fork, local CPU only.

**Population and blind routing.** Pin the released L20 nonlocal `l20-16384-v1` files by release-index and the original 03 rosters (`results/experiment-03-cavity/parent-census.json`). Within each original TRAIN and TEST side separately order immutable (chain seed, chain ID) by ascending SHA256 of UTF-8 `buffer1:{beta_index}:{seed}:{cid}` with unpadded decimal integers. TRAIN ranks 0:6144 fit, 6144:8192 dev. TEST ranks 0:4096 Gate 1 assessment, 4096:8192 Gate 2 reserve. Identities only for the entire roster, no Gate 2 arrays selected. Separately allowed beta-zero de novo fit/dev/assessment for the negative control. Cold 500M nonlocal endpoints only, indices 10/11, 128 each, additional zero-shot tests, IDs/digests from cold-frozen-transfer-320-340. These exams are independent of this fit, not historically untouched. Source/calibration-only frozen checkpoints and roster hashes published before native or exact assessment targets extracted. No target-temperature normalization, recalibration, early stopping or model changes. Future Gate 2 reserve is untouched by this experiment, not virgin across previous work.

**Sample and physics.** 03 lexicographical (0,1,2) sector (2,2,3) on [center,−z,+z,−y,+y,−x,+x]; all eligible stars from the identical species-blind PCG64 [309031,index,seed,ID] random order/shift of 216 disjoint candidate stars. Equal mass to parents with ≥1 eligible star, uniform across their eligible anchors; secondary first eligible only with same fitted decoders. Replacing a chosen interior by *any* state in its sector cannot change eligibility of that star, of the other disjoint interiors, or their order. Thus neither the parent inclusion, the inverse-count anchor weight nor the first-star rule reweights states within that sector. Under the fixed-composition equilibrium law, conditioning on the full exterior and this sampling event gives the exact 210-way Gibbs law q; the tower property gives E[q|masked shell]=P(X|masked shell). No assumption that the archived parents are exactly equilibrated. q uses relative full-H energies, never supplied as covariates; observed X and q are paired measurements on one parent, not independent samples.

**Masks.** Six signed faces (z,y,x; negative then positive), 5×5 contiguous face patches, in-plane coordinates −2..2 in sorted remaining axis order. Primary R4, secondary R5. Audit native sector, orientation/low bits, composition, energy differences on representatives. No direct-contact discovery.

**Controls.** Source contact R2 checks real known dependence (both rich−prior lower bounds >0). A composition-matched R4 shuffled control independently permutes whole shells among *different* parents within each fit/dev/assessment, one prespecified first anchor per parent; group by floor(Nvac/4),floor(NA/4), discard singleton bins, deterministic random cyclic derangement. Fit and score on shuffled shells, report support. Beta-zero canonical: all permutations of fixed center inventory are equiprobable for every eligible exterior by the invariance proof. Score uniform oracle, show native/teacher calibration, and fit de novo at beta0. For shuffled and beta0 controls require upper 95% rich−base <+.01 for both soft and observed.

**Weak plant and off control** (not natural configurations, no MCMC). Use the SAME physical parent rosters, grouped shell views, four-count composition bins. If a conventional model can also exploit that feature, report how much rather than declaring it beyond baseline by fiat.

**Inference and one decision.** For each independent assessment parent average the 210-state exact expected and one-native-state log2 score; compute rich−base and their paired soft−native discrepancy. Report model absolute scores, calibration and category support, source-to-target independent contrasts, full and first-star views, nested quarter/half/full precision, temperature and gap curves. Bootstrap 2048 whole parents within cohort; compare independent source and target draws. Never use stars, optimizer starts or resampling repeats as independent physical observations. Signed mean and Kish support of absolute signed parent contributions. Primary intersection rule for R4 source **and** prespecified index 8 transfer: lower two-sided 95% soft gain >.01 bit/star AND native gain lower >0, paired discrepancy interval wholly within ±.01 and effective support ≥100 for gains; AND all mandatory controls pass. .01 bit corresponds to 1.007× geometric probability per native star; below this we do not promote this expensive candidate. Intersection–union across required positive findings; no picking a gap, temperature, seed or proxy after scoring. Failure of a required control -> failed control / inconclusive; failed precision -> underpowered; only sufficiently sensitive and precise tests can exclude the chosen effect for this setup. No length, new phase or generic physical null. Even on PASS send to supervisor and stop, no Gate 2.
